# Economy

A simple coin economy, separate in every server: a wallet, a bank and a few ways to earn.

- `/economy` creates your account in this server, or deletes it again.
- `/bal` shows your wallet, bank and total.
- `/beg` wins or loses 10 to 310 coins, once a minute.
- `/deposit amount` and `/withdraw amount` move coins between wallet and bank. Use `all` to move everything.

## Vote rewards

Voting for Doubt on top.gg pays **250 coins** into the wallet of every economy account you have, **doubled on weekends**. You can vote again every 12 hours, and Doubt sends you a thank-you DM if your DMs are open.

<Callout>
Coins have no value outside Doubt and can't be bought, sold or traded for anything real.
</Callout>
