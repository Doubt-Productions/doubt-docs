# Levelling

Members earn XP for chatting and level up over time. Levelling is on in every server unless you turn it off in `/setup` → **Levelling**.

## How XP works

- One message a minute per member earns **15 to 25 XP**. Messages in between earn nothing, so spamming doesn't help.
- Going from level `L` to the next level costs `5L² + 50L + 100` XP. Reaching level 1 takes 100 XP; going from level 9 to 10 takes 955 XP, and each level after that costs a little more.
- Doubt only notes who sent a message and where. It never reads or stores the text for levelling.

## Rank cards and leaderboard

- `/rank info` shows your rank card (or someone else's with `user`): level, XP into the level, progress bar and rank in the server.
- `/rank leaderboard` shows the members with the most XP.

## Level-up messages

Pick where they go in `/setup` → **Levelling**: **where they chatted**, **in one channel**, or off. The default message is:

> 🎉 @member reached level **5**!

Placeholders: `{user}` (a mention), `{username}`, `{level}` and `{server}`.

## Reward roles

`/rank reward add level role` gives a role when a member reaches that level; `/rank reward remove level` stops it. A server can have **25** reward roles, or **100** with Premium. Reward roles follow the same [safety rules](/features/welcome#roles-doubt-may-give) as welcome roles.

## Changing someone's level

`/rank set user level` and `/rank reset user` change or clear a member's level. Both need **Manage Server**.

## Premium extras

<Callout type="premium" title="💎 Server Premium: XP boosts and more rewards">
In `/setup` → **Levelling** → **XP boosts** (or on the dashboard), give roles and channels or categories a multiplier from 1.1× to 3×, up to 25 of each, and pick up to 25 channels or categories where messages earn no XP. A message earns the member's best role boost times the best channel boost, capped at 3×. Servers with Premium can also have 100 reward roles.
</Callout>

<Callout type="premium" title="💎 User Premium: your own rank card">
`/rank style` picks one of eight backgrounds, an accent colour and a bar colour for your rank card, in every server. `/rank style reset:true` goes back to the standard card.
</Callout>

If Premium ends, boosts and styles are kept but not used until it's back, and only the 25 lowest-level rewards are given.
