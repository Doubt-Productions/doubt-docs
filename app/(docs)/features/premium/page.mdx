# Premium

Doubt Premium comes in two kinds, both sold through Discord's own shop. Moderation, AutoMod, reports and tickets stay free for everyone.

| | Server Premium | User Premium |
|---|---|---|
| **Who buys it** | A server admin, for one server | Anyone, for themselves |
| **How it's paid** | A monthly subscription | A one-time purchase |
| **Works in** | That server | Every server you share with Doubt |

Server Premium gives the person who pays for it no User Premium perks, and the other way round.

## Server Premium

| Perk | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Custom bot look** | Doubt's own avatar, banner and About Me in your server: `/botlook avatar`, `/botlook banner`, `/botlook bio`, or the dashboard's **Bot look** page. |
| **Custom ticket topics** | Up to 10 ticket topics of your own, each with a name, emoji and staff role. See [Tickets](/features/tickets#topics). |
| **XP boosts** | More XP for chosen roles and channels, and channels with no XP. See [Levelling](/features/levelling#premium-extras). |
| **More level rewards** | Up to 100 level reward roles instead of 25. |
| **Scheduled messages** | Post embeds once or on repeat with `/schedule`. See [Utility](/features/utility#embed-creator). |
| **Dashboard stats** | Ticket and case trends over 30 or 90 days, and a CSV export of every case. |
| **Welcome image cards** | A picture card with the new member's avatar on every welcome message. |

## User Premium

| Perk | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Custom rank card** | Pick your rank card's background, colours and bar with `/rank style`, in every server. |
| **Profile flair** | An accent colour and a short bio line on your `/user info` and `/user profile`. |
| **Premium badge** | A 💎 Premium badge on your profile. |
| **Longer AFK messages** | AFK messages up to 500 characters instead of 200. |

## Getting Premium

Run `/premium` in your server. It shows what's active for you and for the server, with Discord's buy buttons for whatever isn't. You can also open Doubt's profile in Discord and go to its store, or read more on [doubtbot.eu/premium](https://doubtbot.eu/premium).

Payments go through Discord: Doubt never sees or stores your payment details. Cancel a server subscription in Discord under **User Settings → Subscriptions**.

## When Premium ends

Nothing is deleted. Your custom topics, boosts, schedules, styles and flair are kept and come back when Premium does. While it's off:

- Doubt goes back to its normal avatar, banner and bio in the server.
- The ticket panel shows the standard topics; open tickets keep working.
- Scheduled messages are paused, and only the 25 lowest-level reward roles are given.
